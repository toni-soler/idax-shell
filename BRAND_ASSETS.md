# IDAX brand assets

The canonical IDAX product artwork is versioned under `brand/idax`. The horizontal logo is the approved product identity; `logo-mark.svg` is its small-format mark. Web raster derivatives are generated deterministically with `brand/generate_web_assets.py` and must not be edited manually.

The existing distribution used the parent iDynamicsAX identity. It is a valid related mark, but not the canonical IDAX product logo. Consumers should migrate to these assets without copying or modifying the SVG originals.
