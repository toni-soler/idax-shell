## Gate result

Core-consuming architecture is clear. Real Platform/Frontend source promotion
is blocked until the copyright owner explicitly authorizes Apache-2.0
relicensing (including contributions committed under organizational and AI
service identities). No manifest for those files is approved before that gate.
