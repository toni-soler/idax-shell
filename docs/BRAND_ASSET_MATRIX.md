## Audit conclusions

The distribution copies of `logo.svg` and `login-logo.svg` are byte-identical to the iDynamicsAX reverse logo (`ea236d…cff2a2d`), not the IDAX product logo. Its `favicon.svg` is byte-identical to the parent iDynamicsAX mark (`429127…74189`). The distribution PNGs are lower-resolution derivatives and are not authorities. The canonical IDAX product SVG above should update both distribution and Shell consumers through a deterministic asset-copy/build step.

The historical osTRIS/STIR directory contains four opaque 1008×1008 raster concepts. They materially conflict with the current book identity and have no vector source, so they are classified as legacy and were not copied. The current book generator was found and establishes the exact palette: `#173F3B`, `#F4F0E5`, `#D59B3D`, `#2D5B57`, `#15201F`, and `#9CB6B1`.
